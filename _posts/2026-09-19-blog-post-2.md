Below are some resources I found useful for academic writing and speaking. They range from articles, books and an online course.

*  [10 tips for academic talks ](https://matt.might.net/articles/academic-presentation-tips/)
*  [Clinical Chemistry guide to scientific writing](https://academic.oup.com/clinchem/pages/guide-to-scientific-writing?login=true)
*  [How to write a paper](https://staff.fnwi.uva.nl/u.endriss/how-to/write-a-paper/) 
*  [How to give a talk](https://staff.fnwi.uva.nl/u.endriss/how-to/give-a-talk/) 
*  [Writing in the Sciences](https://www.coursera.org/learn/sciwrite)
*  [On writing well](https://www.amazon.com/Writing-Well-30th-Anniversary-Nonfiction-ebook/dp/B0090RVGW0/ref=books_amazonstores_desktop_mfs_author_smart_catalog_unrecognized_1?_encoding=UTF8&pd_rd_w=xE72x&content-id=amzn1.sym.47218010-ccda-4098-a91e-bc39e6d446f4&pf_rd_p=47218010-ccda-4098-a91e-bc39e6d446f4&pf_rd_r=136-6283548-4658667&pd_rd_wg=7ubFR&pd_rd_r=7bfd92aa-0afb-4e43-a5d8-ed0ad99d8e8b) This book by William Zinsser is one of my favorites about writing nonfiction.
*  [Even a geek can speak](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0978577604)

For those wondering about AI and writing, Stven Mintz's take might be interesting.
* [Learning to Write Like AI](https://stevenmintz.substack.com/p/learning-to-write-like-ai)
* [How AI Critiques an Argument](https://stevenmintz.substack.com/p/how-ai-critiques-an-argument)
